# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, recruiters, founders, and technical collaborators evaluating Sean Jones for leadership and high-leverage engineering work. Most users are scanning quickly first, then drilling into proof of execution through projects, skills, and work history.

## Product Purpose

Present a high-credibility narrative of Sean Jones as a Director of Engineering who can lead teams and ship complex products across frontend, backend, AI, and platform surfaces. Success means visitors quickly understand capability, trust delivery history, and have a clear next action to connect.

## Brand Personality

Confident, pragmatic, senior.
Bold in choices where it improves clarity, but always technically grounded and direct.

## Anti-references

- Template SaaS card-grid look with generic icon blocks and repetitive scaffolding
- Corporate sterile resume-page aesthetic that feels static and low-signal

## Design Principles

1. Lead with proof, not claims: every section should show concrete outcomes, not vague statements.
2. Senior-level signal density: concise copy, intentional hierarchy, and no decorative clutter.
3. Distinctive but usable: visual personality is welcome when it improves memorability and scan speed.
4. Narrative flow over component sameness: each section should have a clear role in the career story.
5. Action clarity: every major section should naturally orient users toward the next decision or contact path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA baseline across the site.
Support keyboard navigation for all interactive UI, maintain visible focus states, provide meaningful alt text for non-decorative imagery, and avoid motion patterns that hinder readability or control.
